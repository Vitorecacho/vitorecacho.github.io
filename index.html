Vitor Recacho

PhD candidate in Economics, University of São Paulo (FEA-RP) · Visiting researcher, ITC, University of Twente

I build Bayesian spatio-temporal models for environmental data: streamflow on river networks, extreme rainfall, landslides and wildfires, and the economic losses they cause. Most of my work uses latent Gaussian models fitted with INLA and SPDE methods. I am now moving toward spatial extremes and toward how physical climate risk turns into financial risk.

vitorrecacho@usp.br ORCID 0000-0001-5452-0405

Research

Streamflow and river networks

Structural decomposition of streamflow into trend, seasonality and long memory, from single gauges to whole basins, including SPDE models on river-network metric graphs for the Amazon.

Extreme rainfall and natural hazards

Log-Gaussian Cox processes for extreme rainfall in Brazil, joint models for landslides, debris flows and rockfall, and dynamic models of wildfire activity.

Climate risk and economics

Translating hazard models into expected losses, VaR and TVaR, and testing whether markets such as Brazil's subsidized rural insurance price climate risk.
